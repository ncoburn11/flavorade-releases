Weekly update

Adds a new week to the Release Finder. Nothing here is public until you upload the new data.js.

  1. Paste this week's lines below, in the same format as the old Squarespace code: the NEW FLAVORS line and every store line ({ id: '...', name: '...', dropDate: '...', flavors: [...] },). Extra text around them is ignored.
  2. Click Check, then fill in type and tasting notes for any flavor the site hasn't seen before.
  3. Click Download data.js and upload it to the flavorade-releases GitHub repo, replacing the old file. The site updates within a minute or two.
